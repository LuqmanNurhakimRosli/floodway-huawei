## 1. Format rules (confirmed in the call)

| Rule | What was said |
|---|---|
| **One slide or poster only** | Organizers repeated this several times. More than one page will lose points. An early remark that several pages were allowed was corrected. |

| **Remote members** | A member who cannot attend should contact the organizer. The organizer will decide case by case. |
| **Demo or video** | Not required. A demo can raise your chances, since video teams have often scored higher, but the one-page rule means no video in the preliminary round. |

## 2. How judging works

- **Presentation quality matters most.** The organizers said they are judging the ideas and the presentation, not checking rule compliance.
- **Uniqueness** of the problem and the solution is vital.
- **Feasibility.** The idea should be realistic, with signs that it is already being built or researched. Something impossible would be rejected.
- **Affordability for society** is valued. A low-cost product that helps many people is worth discussing. Having a finished product is not required; a good idea and a good presentation are enough.
- **Format penalties** were described as small compared with presentation quality.

## 3. Structure advice

- Break the slide into sections: **problem, solution, impact**.
- Keep it clear, entertaining and fast.
- Have a reliable internet connection.

## 4. Timing

- The presentation is about **five minutes**.
- Results may come quickly, possibly the same day. No firm date was given for the semi-final.
