FloodWay starts with a warning. Before residents even open the app, an alert reaches their phone, notifying them that Sungai Klang near Masjid Jamek has reached warning level.

The warning combines four factors: water depth from IoT sensors, MetMalaysia rainfall forecasts, exposed-area mapping, and street camera readings. Each component is labelled to distinguish what works today from what is still planned.

Here, the camera detects water at 78 per cent of the riverbank level, and rising, signalling worsening conditions.

Residents can then find nearby relief centres, with open or full status displayed. Availability is entered manually in this demo, with live operator integration planned.

Once a shelter is selected, FloodWay generates a route designed to avoid flooded roads. The journey is compressed into five seconds for this demonstration.

Residents can also submit reports using a photo, description, and location. Gemini 2.5 Flash analyses the image and suggests a result, but AI alone cannot verify or publish the report.

An operator reviews the evidence and approves the submission. Only human-verified reports update the flood map and help recalculate safer routes.

Finally, a Telegram message shares the warning, recommended shelter, and route with the family group.

FloodWay connects flood alerts, community reporting, human verification, and evacuation guidance in one workflow, helping families respond with greater confidence.
